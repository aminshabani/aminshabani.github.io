        \section{Research Summary}
        {\small I build generative and structured-prediction models whose outputs are \emph{discrete and constrained} --- vector floorplans, editable layouts, spatial arrangements, ranked evidence sets --- rather than free-form pixels or text. Recurring threads: diffusion over vector and geometric representations under exact relational constraints; transformers whose attention is shaped by problem structure; and LLM systems built to the accuracy, latency, and privacy budgets of real deployments. 11 peer-reviewed papers (CVPR $\times$2, ICLR $\times$2, NeurIPS, ICCV, ACL, BMVC, ACCV) and 2 US patent applications.}

            {\small LLM systems for finance, from prototype to production. Two lines of work, both published and open-sourced:}
            \begin{itemize}
                \item {\small \textbf{Agentic table summarization} (\href{https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.453.pdf}{FACTS}, ACL Findings 2026; \href{https://github.com/BorealisAI/FACTS}{code}). Recast query-focused table summarization as \emph{offline template generation}: an agentic workflow synthesizes guiding questions, SQL, and a Jinja2 template, each stage validated by an \emph{LLM Council} ensemble. Only schemas --- never records --- reach the LLM, and reusable templates keep runtime flat in table size. 97\% intent match, 94\% SQL accuracy; best or second-best on every metric across FeTaQA, QTSumm, QFMTS.}
                \item {\small \textbf{Efficient context-aware reranking} (\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13329}{EBCAR}, ICLR 2026; \href{https://github.com/BorealisAI/EBCAR}{code}). A 126M-parameter reranker running entirely on pre-computed passage embeddings: document-ID and position encodings plus hybrid full/document-masked attention capture cross-document evidence and intra-document coreference. Best average nDCG@10 (64.92) on ConTEB at 29.33 queries/s on one A100 --- an order of magnitude faster than LLM-based listwise rerankers.}
                \item {\small \textbf{1st place}, Financial Agentic Retrieval Grand Challenge (\href{https://ai4f.org/2025-challenge}{FinAgentBench}), AI for Finance Symposium 2025.}
            \end{itemize}
