\section{Research Summary}
I build generative and structured-prediction models whose outputs are \emph{discrete and constrained} — vector floorplans, editable layouts, spatial arrangements, ranked evidence sets — rather than free-form pixels or text. Recurring threads: diffusion over vector and geometric representations under exact relational constraints; transformers whose attention is shaped by problem structure; and LLM systems built to the accuracy, latency, and privacy budgets of real deployments. 11 peer-reviewed papers (CVPR $\times$2, ICLR $\times$2, NeurIPS, ICCV, ACL, BMVC, ACCV) and 2 US patent applications.

\entry{Machine Learning Researcher}{\href{https://rbcborealis.com/}{RBC Borealis}, Vancouver}{Apr 2024 -- Jun 2026}
\small LLM systems for finance, from prototype to production. Two lines of work, both published and open-sourced:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Agentic table summarization} (\href{https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.453.pdf}{FACTS}, ACL Findings 2026; \href{https://github.com/BorealisAI/FACTS}{code}). Recast query-focused summarization as offline template generation: an agentic workflow synthesizes guiding questions, SQL, and Jinja2 templates, each validated by an LLM-council ensemble. Only schemas reach the LLM and reusable templates keep runtime flat in table size. 97\% intent match, 94\% SQL accuracy; best or second-best on every metric across FeTaQA, QTSumm, QFMTS.
  \item \textbf{Efficient context-aware reranking} (\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13329}{EBCAR}, ICLR 2026; \href{https://github.com/BorealisAI/EBCAR}{code}). A 126M-parameter reranker over pre-computed passage embeddings; document-ID and position encodings with hybrid full/document-masked attention capture cross-document evidence and coreference. Best average nDCG@10 (64.92) on ConTEB at 29.3 queries/s on one A100, an order of magnitude faster than LLM listwise rerankers.
  \item \textbf{1st place}, Financial Agentic Retrieval Grand Challenge (\href{https://ai4f.org/2025-challenge}{FinAgentBench}), AI for Finance Symposium 2025.
\end{itemize}\normalsize

\entry{Research Scientist/Engineer Intern}{\href{https://research.adobe.com/}{Adobe Research}, San Jose}{Jun 2023 -- Nov 2023}
\small Developed \href{https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Shabani_Visual_Layout_Composer_Image-Vector_Dual_Diffusion_Model_for_Design_Layout_CVPR_2024_paper.html}{Visual Layout Composer} (CVPR 2024): an image diffusion model and a vector diffusion model over element boxes denoise in lockstep through cross-domain attention, so element appearance informs geometry while the output stays a layered, editable design. State of the art on multiple layout benchmarks; filed as a US patent application.\normalsize
